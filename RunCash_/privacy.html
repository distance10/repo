Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

RunCast does not collect, store, or share personal data. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

Location

When you grant permission, RunCast uses your current location solely to request weather data from Apple WeatherKit. The location is processed in memory, is never stored, and is never sent anywhere except Apple WeatherKit. You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Weather Data

Weather information is fetched from Apple WeatherKit and is processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Local Storage

Your wind and rain thresholds are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded.

No Tracking

RunCast contains no ads, no trackers, and does not link any data to your identity.

Children

RunCast is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.

Contact

Questions? Email distaner@icloud.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月

概述

RunCast 不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人数据。无账号、无广告、无分析统计。

位置信息

在你授权后，RunCast 仅使用当前位置向 Apple WeatherKit 请求天气数据。位置只在内存中处理，绝不存储，除 Apple WeatherKit 外不发送给任何服务器。你可随时在系统设置中撤销授权。

天气数据

天气信息来自 Apple WeatherKit，由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

本地存储

你的风速与降雨阈值仅存储在本机，绝不上传。

不追踪

RunCast 无广告、无追踪器，不将任何数据与你的身份关联。

儿童

RunCast 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集其数据。

联系方式

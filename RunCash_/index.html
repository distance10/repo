Welcome to RunCast Support

RunCast tells runners the best moment to head out. It checks minute-by-minute rain and hourly wind for the next 6 hours and gives you a clear verdict: Go now, leave in 20 minutes, or wait a while longer.

FAQ

How does RunCast pick the best time to run?

It analyzes minute-level rain and hourly wind for the next 6 hours against your personal thresholds, then gives a clear verdict such as Go now or Leave in 20 minutes.

Where does the weather data come from?

Apple WeatherKit — the same data source used by the built-in Weather app.

Can I adjust the thresholds?

Yes. In Settings you can tune the maximum wind speed (10–60 km/h) and rain intensity (0.1–5 mm/h) to your comfort.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, and Chinese.

Is my location stored or shared?

No. Your location is used only to fetch weather from Apple WeatherKit. It is never stored on any server or shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
